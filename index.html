Controls

  • Space flip your pole N ⇄ S
  • Z undo · R restart · H hint · [ ] rooms

How magnets work here

  • Your pulse travels in a straight line and grabs the first body it meets. Walls stop it; glass does not.
  • Unlike poles pull, like poles push. Plain iron has no pole: it is always pulled, never pushed.
  • Force is mutual. Light bodies slide to you or away from you until something stops them. Heavy bodies (riveted anchors, glowing pillars) move you instead — you fly until something stops you.
  • Push a block that cannot move and the push comes back: you recoil.
  • A block that slides into a pit sinks and fills it. If you stop over a pit, you fall.
  • Plates hold blast doors open only while something rests on every plate.
