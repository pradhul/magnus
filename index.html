MAGNUS Chapter 1

Controls

  • ←↑↓→ / WASD walk
  • Shift + direction — pulse (or tap a tile in your row/column)
  • Space flip your pole N ⇄ S
  • Z undo · R restart · I info · [ ] rooms
  • On a phone or in fullscreen, use the on-screen pad. Pulse mode makes the pad pulse instead of walk.

How magnets work here

  • Metal glows. Anything magnetic has a shining ring at its base — white for iron, red or blue for a pole. Rocks, logs and hedges have no ring and do nothing. Bodies bolted to a riveted steel plate are heavy: they never slide, they move you.
  • Your pulse travels in a straight line and grabs the first body it meets. Rocks stop it; crystal does not.
  • Unlike poles pull, like poles push. Plain iron has no pole: it is always pulled, never pushed.
  • Force is mutual. Light bodies slide to you or away from you until something stops them. Heavy bodies (riveted steel anchors, lodestones) move you instead — you fly until something stops you.
  • Push a block that cannot move and the push comes back: you recoil.
  • A block that slides into water sinks and fills it. If you stop over water, you fall.
  • Stone plates hold wooden gates open only while something rests on every plate.

Legend

  • grass · rock / hedge · water
  • crystal · steel anchor (heavy iron, Fe)
  • N · S — blocks (light, slide) and lodestones (heavy, bolted down)
  • iron crate (Fe) · plate · gate · lantern
