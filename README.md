# Magnus

A 3D puzzle-platformer about a salvage rigger whose body became a magnet.

Magnus can **latch onto** ferrous metal and **push away** from it. Force is mutual: heavy things move *him*, light things *he* moves. Wood, glass, plastic, and rubber do nothing.

## Level 1 — The Pull

**Setting:** Loading Bay 0 at the Polar Foundry, after an electromagnet surge during a crane test.

**Goal:** Reach the control booth before the bay locks.

| Beat | What you learn |
| --- | --- |
| The twitch | Scrap leans toward his hands. Walk. |
| Light crate | Attract pulls the *crate* to him. |
| Heavy girder | Attract pulls *Magnus* across a gap. |
| Dead material | Plastic catwalk. Only steel rivets latch. |
| Repel pad | Same poles launch him over a chemical pool. |
| Wrecking ball | Attract is not always the answer. |
| Maglev corridor | Repel both rails and float. |
| Cycling crane | Ride flipping polarity up to the booth. |

## Movement (Level 1)

- **Walk** — normal movement on non-metal
- **Attract** — opposite poles; latch to heavy metal or pull light metal in
- **Repel** — same poles; launch off heavy plates or kick light objects away
- **Release** — drop a latch mid-flight

Combat, wall-walking, and flipping other objects' poles are out of scope for Level 1.
